Reads lab-results.js from the same folder as this page — opens straight from disk, no server, and nothing is ever sent anywhere.

Reading this page: each card is one lab test tracked over time. The shaded band (or dashed line) is the lab’s normal reference range; a diamond marker means that result was flagged outside range. Hover any point for the exact date and value. The table at the bottom holds one-off tests, screening panels, and qualitative findings that don’t have enough repeat numeric measurements for a trend line.
